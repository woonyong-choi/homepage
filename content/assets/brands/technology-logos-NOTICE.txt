Treatment: icon-only variants are selected from original project distributions;
geometry and palette are retained and proportional fit/centering is applied.
The Kotlin, C#, and Node.js gradients are converted to static paint steps for
the SVG renderer. Node.js uses 512 sRGB samples per gradient and masks for the
original clipped faces; the source geometry and gradient stops remain the
comparison reference. Neutral glyph parts use the light theme neutral ink of
design-tokens. No extra decorative background is added.

not an Ecma-designated official mark. https://github.com/voodootikigod/logo.js
